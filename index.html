Privacy Policy for BrokersBlitz

Effective Date: November 14, 2026 | Last Updated: November 14, 2026

🛡️ Privacy Highlights at a Glance

BrokersBlitz is built with an Offline-First & Zero-Knowledge Architecture. All client leads, property inventory, vehicle stock, loan/insurance records, commission records, and follow-up notes are saved exclusively in a private SQLite database directly on your personal device. We do not collect, upload, sell, monetize, or transmit your business data or client information to any external server or third party.

1. Introduction & Applicability

This Privacy Policy applies to the BrokersBlitz mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application" or "App"), package name com.adarsh9926.BrokersBlitz, developed and maintained by Adarsh Dhote (hereinafter referred to as "Developer", "we", "us", or "our").

BrokersBlitz is a productivity toolkit designed for Indian independent brokers, real estate consultants, automotive dealers, loan direct selling agents (DSAs), and insurance point of sales persons (POSPs) to manage inventory, track prospective buyers/tenants, compute brokerage/EMI figures, and set follow-up tasks.

2. Information Collection and Storage

The Application operates locally on your Android device. The categories of information handled include:

A. User-Entered Business & Client Data (Stored Locally Only)

  • Client & Lead Information: Client name, telephone/mobile number, email address, profession/occupation, budget parameters, property/vehicle preferences, lead status, priority, and source.
  • Inventory & Listings Data: Property, vehicle, loan, or insurance specifications, pricing units (Thousands/Lakhs/Crores), address/locality details, and owner/landlord contact telephone numbers.
  • Financial & Commission Records: Commission models, expected deal earnings, loan amounts, tenures, interest rates, and custom quote notes.
  • Activity & Task Notes: Reminder dates, site visit schedules, notes, and local notification items.
Storage Technology: All user-entered data is written exclusively to an encrypted/sandboxed SQLite database (via expo-sqlite) within the application's isolated private filesystem on your Android device. No copies are sent to external databases or cloud servers.

B. Device and Technical Information

The Application does not collect hardware identifiers, IMEI numbers, biometric details, or exact GPS locations. Standard Android system attributes (such as screen size, dark/light theme setting, and operating system API level) are read in-memory solely to render the user interface adaptively.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies, we disclose all permissions requested by the Application and the explicit purpose of each:

Permission Purpose Data Transmission
android.permission.INTERNET Required for standard network socket functionality (e.g. verifying connectivity status and opening external user-initiated web links). No personal or client data is transmitted over the internet.
android.permission.POST_NOTIFICATIONS / VIBRATE Used exclusively to deliver scheduled local device alarms and reminder banners when an upcoming client follow-up or site visit is due. Strictly local on-device notifications. No remote push notification tokens or servers are involved.
android.intent.action.VIEW (Phone & WhatsApp Intents) Allows you to tap the Call or WhatsApp button on a lead or inventory card to launch your device's native dialer (tel:) or WhatsApp application with pre-filled text. The action is initiated entirely by your explicit tap and processed by third-party apps installed on your device.

4. Third-Party Services & Libraries

The Application is built using the React Native and Expo frameworks. The third-party libraries integrated within the Application comply with the following boundaries:

  • Google Play Services: Used strictly for fundamental OS compatibility, app integrity verification, and runtime libraries.
  • No Advertising SDKs: BrokersBlitz does not contain Google AdMob, Unity Ads, Facebook Audience Network, or any other commercial advertising networks. No advertising identifiers (AAID) are tracked or monetized.
  • No Tracking Analytics: We do not integrate third-party behavior tracking or session recording tools (such as Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, or AppsFlyer).

5. Data Retention & User Data Deletion (Google Play Compliance)

Because we do not store your data on remote servers or cloud databases, you maintain 100% control over the retention and deletion of your data at all times:

  • Granular Deletion: You can edit or permanently delete any individual lead, inventory listing, or notification directly from within the app interfaces at any moment.
  • Factory Reset (Full Data Purge): You can navigate to More > Factory Reset Data within the Application to immediately wipe all SQLite tables (leads, inventory, notifications) in one tap.
  • Uninstallation: Deleting or uninstalling the BrokersBlitz application from your device permanently and irreversibly erases all local SQLite database files, preferences, and cache from your device storage.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

BrokersBlitz is a business and commercial productivity tool intended exclusively for licensed or practicing adult brokers, agents, consultants, and business professionals. It is not directed at or designed for children under the age of 13 (or under 18 in relevant jurisdictions). We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children.

7. Data Security

Your data is protected by the native security model of the Android operating system. The SQLite database resides in the Application's private sandboxed directory, preventing other non-rooted apps on the device from accessing or reading your client records. However, users are encouraged to secure their physical device with a passcode, pattern, or biometric lock to prevent unauthorized physical access.
